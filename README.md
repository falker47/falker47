### Ringmin

![Computational research](https://img.shields.io/badge/Computational_research-8B5CF6?style=flat-square)
![Two-paper program](https://img.shields.io/badge/Two--paper_program-B31B1B?style=flat-square&logo=arxiv&logoColor=white)

Research on the minimum central-circle problem, from certified finite optima to a global asymptotic theory and an effective characterization of its limiting constant.
